Atomic Orbitals Visualizer With Custom Superpositions
Visualizer of atomic orbitals using volumetric ray tracing, supports raw spherical harmonics, real valued orbitals, and customizable amplitude expressions for different superpositions.
Solar Eclipse simulator with real data from NASA
A simulator for solar eclipses with real astronomy data from NASA. Come see it if you missed it due to clouds or work!
Chaos Attractor Simulator in VR
A simulator for 3D chaos equations where you can customize the equations and particles. Enter to experience in full VR where you can observe the trajectory graph from all angles. Compatible with desktop, headsets, and mobile.
Schrodinger's Sandbox
Interactive simulator of the progression of a quantum wave function by numerically solving the Schrodinger's equation in 1D. Users can input a custom potential energy function and initial wave function.
